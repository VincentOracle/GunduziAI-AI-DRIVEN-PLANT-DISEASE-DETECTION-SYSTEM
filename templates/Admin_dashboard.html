Existing Plant Diseases

Tomato Blight

Symptoms: Brown spots on leaves, wilting, fruit rot.

Preventive Measures: Rotate crops, use resistant varieties.

Chemical Treatments: Fungicides like chlorothalonil.

Organic Solutions: Neem oil, copper sprays.

Best Farming Practices: Proper spacing, avoid overhead watering.

Powdery Mildew

Symptoms: White powdery spots on leaves and stems.

Preventive Measures: Improve air circulation, reduce humidity.

Chemical Treatments: Sulfur-based fungicides.

Organic Solutions: Baking soda spray, milk solution.

Best Farming Practices: Prune infected parts, avoid overcrowding.
